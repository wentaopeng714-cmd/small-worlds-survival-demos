# Artwork and software sources

All selected 3D artwork and character animation clips are from Kay Lousberg / KayKit free CC0 packs. Original notices are retained in `licenses/`.

- [KayKit Adventurers](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0): character geometry and materials.
- [KayKit Character Animations](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations): real compatible animation clips. The included lightweight character GLBs contain 20 clips, with preset handheld weapons removed.
- [KayKit Forest](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest): trees, bushes and rocks.
- [KayKit Medieval Hexagon](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0): buildings and props.
- [KayKit RPG Tools](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/rpg-tools-bits): tool meshes attached to actual hand bones.
- [KayKit Resources](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/resource-bits): logs, planks, stone and ore.
- [KayKit Space Base](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Space-Base-Bits-1.0): solar panel, battery proxy and habitat.
- [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three): MIT, renderer and official GLTFLoader / SkeletonUtils / RoundedBoxGeometry.

Original work in this project: game rules, progression, interaction UI, recoloring shaders, head accessories, assembled environments, bridges and planters, effects, Web Audio synthesis, and result-card generation. No third-party music recordings are included.
